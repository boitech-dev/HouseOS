# Linen Morning → a Japanese zen theme (rename it: e.g. "Kinari"; keep the id linen-morning)

**Idea:** a quiet Japanese room in the morning. Unbleached cotton, washi paper, kraft, dark walnut
and sumi ink, with order and space. Muji's calm authority: every object has its place, nothing
shouts, and the one red is a small hanko seal.

**Tells:**
- **Surfaces and space:** shoji grids and their soft diffused light; tatami proportions (2:1) and
  the dark cloth edge (heri); ma, the meaningful empty space.
- **Marks:** an ensō brush circle; kamon-like crests, simple and circular.
- **Materials:** washi paper fibres; kraft card labels (Muji); dark walnut wood; sumi ink wash
  with soft gradients and bleeding edges.
- **Printing:** a vermilion hanko seal, small and square; print-like typography.

**Palette:**
- kinari beige (the ground, around L 0.92, never pure white)
- washi (surfaces, a touch lighter)
- kraft brown
- walnut
- sumi black (ink and the primary button: a black button with washi text, like a Muji label)
- one hanko vermilion, used tiny: selected marks, the seal on the crest

Light scheme only. It must not read as "white theme": warmth and brown structure.

**Type:**
- **Display:** a mincho-like serif with calligraphic contrast. Check Shippori Mincho, Zen Old
  Mincho or Noto Serif JP on Fontsource have latin-ext for French; otherwise a refined Latin serif.
- **Body:** a calm gothic (Zen Kaku Gothic New or similar).
- **Mono:** quiet.
- **Tracking:** generous on labels.

**Shape and motion:**
- **Shape:** small radii (paper, not pills). Hairline edges in walnut or brown. Panels like
  paper on wood.
- **Motion:** slower and softer (`dur` longer, gentle ease).

**Art** (SVG with turbulence, blur and grain via `svg.py`; a few Blender paper or wood renders
if useful):
- **Home hero:** ink-wash mountains in morning mist, sumi on washi, with lots of empty space. A
  mist layer drifts very slowly across it.
- **Page:** washi fibre texture, very subtle. The shadow of bamboo leaves drifts across as if on
  a shoji screen: a slow page layer (`blend: multiply`, low opacity, maybe `depth` for a gentle
  parallax).
- **Status strip:** a tatami heri band.
- **Rail:** a shoji panel surface. At its foot, a hanging scroll with an ensō.
- **Header banners per room:** calm compositions; a single object or mark per room.
- **Music deck:** a lacquer-tray or kraft-card skin.
- **Crest:** a kamon in sumi with a hanko.
- **Empty state:** an ensō or a single stone.
- **TV bezel:** a walnut frame.
- **My Space:** a tatami room corner.

**Pieces:**
- **Nox:** a minimal cat drawn like a brush mark, 6 moods.
- **Medals:** kamon-style circular crests.
- **Room marks:** simple brush-like glyphs.

**Discoveries:** the leaf shadows moving; the hanko seal on the crest; a single petal or leaf
falling very rarely.

**Never:** Japanese text or kanji in pictures (words come from the interface), cherry-blossom
clichés everywhere, pure white, a pink palette, anime characters.
