# Bathhouse After Hours (sento-night): direction

**Idea:** a Shōwa-era neighbourhood sentō at closing time. Steam, wet mosaic tile, the great Fuji
mural above the baths, a vermilion noren curtain, hinoki wood, the glow of a milk fridge, a cat
asleep on the counter. Warm, quiet and full of little things.

**Tells:**
- **The mural:** the Mt Fuji wall painting (bold, flat, blue and white, with a red sun or a
  pine), the painting every sentō has.
- **The room:** small square mosaic tiles in teal and white with dark grout; the bandai (the
  attendant's high counter).
- **Objects:** wooden shoe lockers with wooden keys; yellow plastic bath basins (generic, never a
  brand); a glass fridge of coffee-milk bottles; round fans; a scale; a massage chair; a noren with
  a simple hot-spring mark ♨ (drawn as a mark, no text).
- **Air:** steam rising, condensation, lantern light outside.

**Keep:** the id, the name, the colours' spirit (deep teal tile, vermilion, milk cream, hinoki),
the fonts if they still serve, the animated Nox. Rebuild the art at a much higher level of detail
and cohesion.

**Art** (pixel mosaic language; one density):
- **Page:** the Fuji mural as a large, detailed pixel painting behind everything (page backdrop),
  seen through steam.
- **Steam:** rise particles over the page (soft, low opacity) plus a slow drift of vapour.
- **Home hero:** the bathing hall with the mural, the baths, the stools and basins, a steam
  layer, maybe the attendant's counter.
- **Header banner:** the noren curtain swaying (frames), with per-room variants:
  - Listen: a radio on the counter
  - Watch: the old TV in the changing room
  - Games: an arcade cabinet by the entrance
  - House: the fridge and scale
- **Rail:** a hinoki locker-wall skin with wooden keys; at its foot, a basin and stool.
- **Music deck:** the bandai counter or a transistor-radio skin; a steam wisp while music plays.
- **Crest:** the ♨ mark in tile. **Empty state:** the basin. **TV bezel:** the changing-room TV.
- **My Space:** the changing room with lockers.
- **Status strip:** a tile band with the mural's waves.

**Pieces:**
- **Nox:** keep its animated concept; improve every mood.
- **Medals:** 20 sentō objects (a basin, a key tag, a milk bottle, a fan, a towel, a scale…).
- **Avatars and room marks:** redrawn in the tile language.

**Discoveries:**
- the cat asleep on the counter that sometimes flicks its tail
- a droplet falling from the ceiling now and then
- the fridge glow
- a paper crane on a locker

**Never:** words or kanji in pictures (♨ is a pictogram, allowed); brand names on objects; mixed
pixel sizes; text over steam.
