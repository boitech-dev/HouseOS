# Pocket Hatchling: direction

**Idea:** the house as a 90s–2000s keychain pet living on a handmade personal homepage. Glitter,
stickers, pixel badges, tiled backgrounds, blinkies, star cursors (drawn), a guestbook energy. All
held together by a disciplined grid and dark ink outlines so it stays usable. Maximalist
decoration lives in the frame (page, rail, banners, deck); the content areas stay calm and legible.

**Tells:**
- **The pet device:** an egg-shaped plastic toy with a tiny reflective LCD (greenish grey, dark
  segment pixels) and three round buttons. The pet evolves, eats and sleeps.
- **The homepage:** tiled backgrounds (stars, hearts, checkerboards, clouds); 88 × 31 badge
  buttons (here: icons only, no text); pixel dividers (hearts, stars, lace); "under construction"
  stripes as a motif without words; sparkles and bubbly shapes.
- **Kawaii rules:** round, blushing faces, pastel candy colours with saturated highlights, and a
  consistent dark outline.

**Palette:**
- **Candy:** bubblegum pink, mint, lavender, lemon, baby blue.
- **Inks and LCD:** a deep plum or navy outline ink; the LCD grey-green.
- **Accent:** one berry accent for primary actions.

Light scheme; consider a "night mode" dark scheme where the LCD backlight glows (optional, only
if excellent). Readability first: text on solid pastel panels with ink.

**Type:**
- a round bubbly display (Chango? Bagel Fat One? Rubik Bubbles is too much for titles… find a
  cute, bold, legible OFL face)
- a friendly rounded body (Nunito, Quicksand-like, or Lexend)
- a pixel or LCD mono (VT323, Silkscreen, DotGothic16 if latin-ext)

**Art** (pixel, one density, and a lot of it):
- **Music deck** (`deck.surface`, 9-slice): **the pet egg**, the device shell around the player.
  The deck's content area is the LCD. Deck layers: the pet bouncing (frames, `playing`), hearts
  floating when music plays.
- **Page:** a tiled glitter or star background drifting slowly; twinkle sparkle particles; maybe
  floating hearts.
- **Rail:** a sticker-sheet skin, its foot a pixel shrine of badges.
- **Status strip:** a lace or heart divider.
- **Header banners per room:** a different tiled homepage band each (stars for Watch, notes for
  Listen, controller hearts for Games).
- **Home hero:** the pet's world: a tiny room with the egg on a cloud, stickers around.
- **Panels:** maybe a 9-slice with a pixel lace or dotted border and a sticker corner.
- **Crest:** the egg. **Empty state:** the pet asleep. **TV bezel:** a toy TV. **My Space:** the
  pet's room.

**Pieces:**
- **Nox:** the pet itself, 6 moods, blink and bounce.
- **Avatars:** cute redraws of moon, bat, raven, rose, ghost.
- **Medals:** 20 stickers and badges (a heart, a cassette, a star, a strawberry…).
- **Room marks:** LCD icons.

**Discoveries:**
- the pet blinks and sometimes sleeps
- a shooting star crosses the page
- the hearts appear only while music plays
- a sticker peeks from a corner

**Never:** words in pictures (no "welcome" blinkies, no "under construction" text); brand
characters; a Hello-Kitty-like likeness; text on glitter; mixed pixel sizes.
