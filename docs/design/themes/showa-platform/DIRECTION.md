# Shōwa Platform: direction

**Idea:** a small local-line station in the 1960s, on a hot summer afternoon. Cream-and-navy
enamel signs, a signal-red accent, cicadas, the shimmer of heat over the rails, a two-car train
arriving now and then, sunflowers by the fence, a vending machine humming.

**Tells:**
- **Signs and tickets:** enamel station-name boards (navy on cream, with the arrows to the
  neighbouring stations), stamped cardboard tickets with punched holes (the kaisatsu clipper), a
  platform clock.
- **The line:** catenary wires and poles against a big blue sky with cumulus; a two-tone local
  train (cream and red or cream and green); rails, sleepers, ballast; a level-crossing signal and
  its black-and-yellow stripes.
- **Details:** a wooden bench, a timetable board (no text: bars), a sunflower, a straw hat, a
  furin wind bell, a ramune bottle, the vending machine.

**Keep:** the id, the name, the palette's spirit (navy, cream, signal red, summer sky), the
dock and now-bar variants if they work. Rebuild the art in far more detail.

**Art** (pixel, one density):
- **Home hero:** the platform in summer with a big sky.
- **Page layers:**
  - clouds drifting in the sky
  - a train crossing now and then (natural, frames for its wheels, `cross`)
  - heat haze (a subtle drift)
  - catenary wires with a little depth
- **Header banners per room:** enamel-sign-style plates, each with its room's pictogram (no
  words; the room name comes from the font over the banner). Or a view down the line from a
  different spot per room.
- **Panels:** the existing 9-slice panel frame, refined, or a new enamel plate frame.
- **Status strip:** the catenary.
- **Rail:** a platform-pillar skin with a timetable board; at its foot, the vending machine
  glowing.
- **Music deck:** a platform radio or the station's PA box skin; a furin that sways while music
  plays.
- **Crest:** the line's emblem (a winged wheel). **Empty state:** an empty bench with a straw hat.
  **TV bezel:** the waiting-room TV.
- **My Space:** the stationmaster's office.
- **Selected items:** a punched-ticket notch motif.

**Controls:** meters like ticket strips, or the level-crossing stripes as the progress pattern
(`part.meter.pattern`), with care for readability.

**Pieces:**
- **Nox:** a station cat in a little cap, 6 moods.
- **Medals:** 20 station objects (a ticket, a lantern, a whistle, a lunchbox, a ramune bottle…).
- **Avatars and room marks.**

**Discoveries:**
- the train
- the furin swaying while music plays
- a dragonfly crossing
- the level crossing
- the sunflower turning

**Never:** words or kanji in pictures; real railway company logos; mixed pixel sizes; text over
busy sky.
