# Pure: direction

**Idea:** architecture made of light. Black concrete, white light, square openings, and nothing
else. A calm, absolute theme that feels expensive because it is exact. Yin and yang: the dark
scheme is pure black with white lines; the light scheme is its **negative**: warm cream paper
with black ink. It replaces Base in the picker (Base stays as the hidden root every theme
inherits: never edit `themes/base`).

**Tells to carry:**
- Tadao Ando's concrete: form-tie holes on a grid, a slit of light, a square window throwing a
  sharp patch across a wall.
- Swiss grids.
- Hairlines of exactly 1px, square corners, big quiet voids.
- Inverted selection: the chosen item becomes a solid block, white on black or black on cream.
- Light as the only ornament.

**Palette:** no hue.
- **Dark:** pure black canvas, near-black surfaces separated by hairlines (not by grey fills),
  white ink, a warm cream as the rare second tone.
- **Light:** cream paper, black ink, black primary buttons.
- **No red:** red belongs to the Modular theme; Pure stays monochrome and cream. Status colours
  (success, warning, danger) stay clearly readable but desaturated and dignified.

**Type:** a precise grotesk with architectural presence for display (consider Archivo at a wide
width, Schibsted Grotesk, Familjen Grotesk, or any OFL grotesk with character), a highly legible
body, and a quiet mono. Tight tracking on display, generous line height in body.

**Shape and motion:** radius 0 everywhere except true circles (avatars). Flat panels with 1px
edges (parts: panel outlined or flat, header plain or framed). Snappy: 90–140 ms, crisp easing, no
bounce.

**Art (Blender, the concrete and light presets):**
- **Page backdrop:** a dark concrete wall with form-tie holes, lit by one square window's patch
  of light. Keep it very dark and quiet behind the scrim.
- **Page layer:** dust motes floating slowly (`particles`, float), light over the black and ink
  over the paper. Not a crossing patch: big moving shapes read as boxes sliding by.
- **Grain:** a faint `above` layer (opacity ≤ 0.06).
- **Home hero:** an interior: a slit of light across concrete stairs or a wall. A picture worth
  pausing on.
- **Rail:** a surface with a vertical concrete band and a seam of light; its foot, a small
  square window.
- **Music deck:** a black 9-slice skin with hairline and corner ticks, like an architectural
  drawing's frame.
- **Crest:** a square within a square (negative space).
- **Empty state:** a square of light on the floor.
- **The light scheme:** its own pictures, or the same ones inverted and warmed to cream. Design
  it on purpose.

**Pieces:**
- **Nox:** a minimal white pixel figure made of squares, the negative of a shadow, all 6 moods.
- **Medals:** geometric monochrome marks.
- **Room marks:** simple architectural glyphs.

**Discoveries** (ideas, pick or better them): the dust in both schemes; the negative
selection; a single form-tie hole pattern that lines up with the grid.

**Never:** grey on grey, blur and glass, gradients for their own sake, any hue.
