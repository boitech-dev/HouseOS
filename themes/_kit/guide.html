03

The method in six steps

Every theme so far needed a second round after its first review, and got clearly better. Plan for it. Full method: themes/_kit/METHOD.md.

Study→Direction→Plan→Draw→Build & tour→Self-critique→Review round→Fix→Tour↺Hand over

Direction

  • Five to eight tells: details that make the aesthetic recognisable at a glance.
  • One idea, three moments (Home's hero, the music deck, a surprise).
  • Palette with roles, light, type, medium, a never-list.
  • The person says yes before anything is drawn.

Draw with code

  • One make.py regenerates every picture, so a fix is an edit, not a redraw.
  • Draw the hero at least three times; keep the best and say why.
  • Look at the contact sheet after every change.
python3 docs/design/themes/<id>/make.py 
from pixel import *
pal = Palette(sky=ramp("#75b0e3", 5),
              ink="#1f2b4d")
hero, draw = canvas(720, 256)
bands(hero, (0, 0, 720, 150),
      pal["sky"])
bench = grid(BENCH, {"k": pal["ink"]})
hero.paste(bench, (600, 214), bench)
pal.lock(hero, "hero")
save(hero, ART / "hero.png")
{{pic:themes/showa-platform/art/hero.png|Shōwa Platform's Home hero: a country platform under a summer sky, pixel art|pixel}}

Build and tour

  • Every place, both schemes, phone and desktop, French, motion still, sign-in.
  • Fails on broken things: sideways scroll, cut text, a layer over a control, contrast, focus rings.
  • Then open every screenshot. Passing is not the same as good.
node themes/_kit/tour.cjs <id>

Review round

  • A fresh reviewer: another agent, a new session or a person.
  • Give only the direction, the rubric, the common failures and the screenshots. Not your notes or code.
  • Ask for scores, what to keep, located fixes in order, a verdict.

  • Listen banner's radio sits under the search button on a phone: move it left of 75 %
  • The TV bezel is flat next to the hero: add the enamel frame
Verdict: another round

Fix, tour, review again

  • Fix in priority order: edit make.py and the tokens.
  • Tour again, send the new screenshots to a reviewer.
  • Stop when the verdict is ship and the person agrees.
Fix→Tour→Review→another round?→Ship Hand over: DIRECTION.md, make.py, NOTES.md, BRIEF.md, a passing REPORT.md, the cards, names and a short description in English and French.

The vocabulary

The words the kit, the checks and your agent use. Every field and limit: themes/README.md.

Slots

Pictures in fixed places

A slot takes a picture and adds nothing to the layout. Every screen is right with every slot empty or filled.

Layers

Pictures the app moves

A layer is a picture in a place (page, hero, header, rail, rail-foot, status, deck) with one way of moving. It draws over its place's picture unless "under": true, and always under the words. 12 layers and 48 particles at most.

driftA seamless tile that pans: clouds, fog, a grid.
cross + framesA piece crossing now and then: a bat, a train.
particlesOne image, repeated: steam, snow, sparkles.
framesA sprite sheet in place: a torch, a curtain.
The person's motion level
stillfirst frames only; nothing crosses
subtledefault: drift, frames, crossings, half the particles
fullevery particle, and parallax (depth)
Also
playingmoves only while music plays (deck layers)
abovea faint finish over the whole page: grain, scanlines (0.15 at most)
depthfollows the pointer a little, full motion only
Pokes

A piece that answers a click

An anecdote from the world, played once: the gargoyle wakes, the candle flares. Desktop with a mouse only, never at motion still, 6 at most, in empty space, never over controls or text.

Pieces and the only words

Nox's 6 moods, the 5 avatars people pick, the room marks and 20 medals: rows of letters, 16 × 16 at most, painted by the theme's colours. The 20 title names and the star mark are a theme's only words, in English and French. Every other line is HouseOS's own.

06

The art toolkit

No drawing program needed. Four small Python tools in themes/_kit/art/; every bundled theme was drawn with them.

{{pic:themes/sento-night/art/hero-hall.png|Bathhouse After Hours: the bath hall, pixel art|px}}

pixel.py

Hue-shifted ramp(), Palette().lock(), grid() sprites from text, outline(), dither(), sheet() for frames, nine() for 9-slice boxes, scale().

{{pic:themes/y2k/art/page-sky.webp|Millennium Skin: a soft sky, drawn as SVG||600}}

svg.py

Write an SVG, render() it to PNG or WebP at the exact size. Gradients, feTurbulence paper and grain, blur; add_grain(), limit().

{{pic:themes/y2k/art/hero.webp|Millennium Skin: chrome and clear plastic toys, a 3D render||600}}

blender.py

Headless 3D scenes: chrome, brushed metal, plastic, glass, concrete, paper presets; a camera, lights and finish().

sheet.py

A contact sheet of your art at 1× and zoomed. Look at it after every change: if it can't be seen at 1×, it doesn't exist.

Craft rules
  • Pixel art: one pixel scale for the whole theme, whole multiples only; clusters, not noise; 2–4 hue-shifted shades per material.
  • Vector: one stroke weight, consistent corners, grain so flat fills don't look plastic.
  • Renders: one light setup, composed for the box and its anchor.
  • Phones crop banners to the middle: keep the subject in 45–75 % of the width, the right end calm for the buttons.
Every picture
  • Original, drawn for this theme, or the person's own work. No logos, brands or borrowed characters.
  • No words, letters or digits in pictures.
  • Smooth art at least the slot's size, never upscaled.
  • The contrast check can't see pictures: veil the art where text lies.

07

Judge it like an art director

Score each criterion from 1 to 10, then check every common first-round failure. The reviewer uses the same list.

  1. ReadabilityEvery text comfortable at a glance; no text on busy pictures.
  2. UsabilityControls look like controls; selected, focus, disabled obvious; French fits.
  3. CohesionOne palette, one light, one pixel scale or stroke, one voice.
  4. CraftPictures hold up zoomed; compositions made for their box.
  5. DepthEvery part considered; three or more discoveries.
  6. RestraintMotion gentle and optional; effects serve the idea; budgets kept.
  7. Would they love itWhat the person asked for, done better than they imagined.

Common first-round failures

  • One great Home picture, the rest thinBanners, deck, My Space, TV and rail read as placeholders.
  • Details too small to see1–2 px screws, Nox moods that differ by one pixel.
  • Text touching artProps under header buttons, sparkles over words.
  • Phone cropsBanner subjects cut off or orphaned on a phone.
  • Pixel scale driftFractional cover scaling, two pixel sizes side by side.
  • Surfaces that let the page throughA transparent 9-slice centre under text.
  • Layer stacking assumed wrongLayers draw over their place's picture unless under.
  • The room tint left onpart.header.banner-wash: 0 for none.
  • Off-palette leftoversDefault avatars, a selected state in the accent's soft tint.
  • Two drawing systemsNox and the medals drawn unlike the pictures.
  • Motion nobody seesSomething should be alive at first sight, with motion on.
  • A big idea gone timidUse the budget when the idea asks for it; a minimal theme does the opposite.
08

Brainstorm card

Your agent asks one question at a time. Pick a letter or say it in your own words. Four to six answers are usually enough for a direction.

  1. Source: what should the house feel like?

    1. A place: a warm kitchen at 8 a.m.
    2. An era: a 1970s hi-fi catalogue
    3. An object, a film, a game
    4. A picture of mine

  2. Light

    1. Dark, for evenings
    2. Light, for mornings
    3. Both, each designed

  3. Where is it used most?

    1. Phones at night
    2. A kitchen wall tablet
    3. A desktop
    4. Everyone, kids too

  4. How much picture?

    1. Colour, type and texture only
    2. Small pieces and scenes
    3. A picture behind every page
    4. A full world that moves

  5. Who is Nox in this world?

    1. A creature of the place
    2. An object come alive
    3. HouseOS's own, recoloured

  6. Never

    1. Neon and glow
    2. Pastel everything
    3. Heavy texture
    4. A colour I dislike

Then three different directions

  • Vary at least three: value key, temperature, material, edge, type personality, shape, motion, how much picture.
  • Pick one, or a mix.

Each direction shows

  • A name (en, fr) and the idea in one sentence
  • 3–6 swatches with a word each: "moss ground", "ember accent"
  • Display and body faces, material and shape
  • The three moments, and who Nox is
  • One surprising but reasoned choice
Paste into your agent, started in the HouseOS folder
Read themes/AGENT-KIT.md and follow it. Help me make my own HouseOS theme: ask me a few short questions (give me choices I can pick), propose three different directions, then build the one I choose, run every check and the tour, and show me screenshots before we refine it.
09

Share and install

A theme travels as one file. Anyone who lives in the house can design one; an administrator shares it with everyone.

  1. Checktheme_kit check <id> must PASS.
  2. Packtheme_kit pack <id> makes <id>.houseos-theme.
  3. Bring it inMe → Appearance → Your themes → Bring a theme file: your draft.
  4. Wear itLook at it on a real phone and screen, both schemes.
  5. ShareAsk to share it; an administrator shares it in Control Room → Themes.
Commands, from the HouseOS folder
PYTHONPATH=backend python3 -m houseos.theme_kit new <id> --scheme dark
PYTHONPATH=backend python3 -m houseos.theme_kit check <id>
PYTHONPATH=backend python3 -m houseos.theme_kit build
node themes/_kit/tour.cjs <id>
PYTHONPATH=backend python3 -m houseos.theme_kit pack <id>
No terminal?
  • Theme workshop → Remix: colours, type, corners, parts, pictures, pixel pieces and title names, with a live preview and the same checks.
  • Make one with Nox: the in-app studio follows the same method.
  • A shared theme passes the same checks again on the server; its CSS is always rebuilt there.
