# Security

HouseOS runs in your home and holds your household's files, accounts and keys. If you find a
way around its sign-in, its permissions or its network boundary, please report it privately:
on GitHub, open the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**. Please don't
open a public issue for it.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version (`./houseos.sh status`). Fixes go
out as a normal update (`./houseos.sh update`).
